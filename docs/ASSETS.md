# Assets and licenses

Only the assets needed by this rebuild are copied into `public/`. The complete original backups remain separate.

## Fonts

- Source Sans 3 Roman and Italic, from Adobe's official Source Sans repository: https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-sans . SIL Open Font License 1.1 is bundled as `public/assets/fonts/Source-Sans-LICENSE.txt`.
- Oswald Medium, obtained from Google Fonts, replaces the original licensed DIN Condensed Web face. Its SIL Open Font License 1.1 is bundled as `public/assets/fonts/Oswald-OFL.txt`; official source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/oswald .

No Adobe Fonts/Typekit DIN or Proxima Nova binaries are used. Font requests go to local files only.

## Images and documents

`asset-provenance.json` records the original public source URL, local SHA-256 and rights basis for each imported asset. Alexandra's existing professional headshots, WorkWell title graphic, collaboration map, CV, conference presentation and public hospital event flyer are migrated as owner-provided professional materials. Existing contributor and document credits are retained. Public availability is not claimed to transfer third-party rights.

Photographs whose existing filenames and captions identify Unsplash keep those credits. Unsplash's published license permits commercial and noncommercial reuse: https://unsplash.com/license . The rebuild does not sell the photographs or compile a competing photo service.

The publisher-clippings collage from Press and the unattributed question-mark photograph from the curiosity article have no verified reuse license in the source. They are not copied into the publish folder. The Press layout preserves its open left column, and the article listing uses a neutral text panel. Restore these optional images only if rights are confirmed. The original copies remain unchanged in the backup.

External research PDFs are generally kept as their original external links rather than republished without a clear redistribution license. A working link is not a promise that the external server will remain available.

The professional headshots use the already archived 750px image variants for smaller page downloads; their full-resolution original files remain in the unchanged backup.
