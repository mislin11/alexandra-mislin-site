Building trust and gaining influence in complex settings


I am an Associate Professor of Management at American University’s Kogod School of Business. My research examines how people build trust and gain influence in complex organizational and social settings. These interactions involve competing interests, uncertain intentions, and dependence on others’ cooperation. I study how emotions, communication, and judgments of trustworthiness shape people’s ability to advance their goals and sustain productive relationships. Negotiation provides a central setting for this work because its consequences extend beyond the agreement itself to implementation, future exchange, and the development or erosion of trust.

My current research explores these questions through curiosity in negotiation, hidden dissatisfaction after agreement, and human–AI bargaining. Through teaching, writing, and work with professional audiences, I help people apply research to the difficult conversations and working relationships they encounter every day.
